## The fakest green

That is the textbook shape named in `reviewer/SKILL.md`: "an assertion that cannot fail." The real coverage of the absent-key path comes entirely from AC1h/i/j's `key=without` cases and AC1k's structural floor, added later specifically because the tech-lead and qa-specialist caught this exact gap for the *present*-key path — but nobody closed the same hole for AC1a/b itself; qa-specialist's own rec 2 proposed replacing it and the session chose "supplement" over "replace," leaving the hollow pair in place, now outnumbered but not removed.
